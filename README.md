# Featured Projects

## 💰 Philippine Digital Bank After-Tax Yield Analysis

An analysis of how much Philippine digital banks *actually* pay after the 20% final withholding tax on deposit interest — comparing advertised vs. effective yields across banks, balance tiers, and holding periods.

### Highlights

- Cleaned and structured rate data from multiple PH digital banks
- Computed effective after-tax yields under the CMEPA 20% withholding tax
- Compared returns across contribution patterns and balance tiers
- Reproducible pipeline with documented methodology and assumptions
